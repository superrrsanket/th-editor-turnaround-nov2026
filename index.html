My read, not data

This part is Claude’s read of the numbers above. The rest of the page stays data only.

How this was counted
Window17 Aug to 17 Nov 2026, #internal channel threads. Work before 17 Aug is not in here. Mohit started 17 Aug, Sharan 14 Sep, Manish 26 Oct.
AssignedThe day of the assignment message. Where the thread says the video was queued behind another one, or the brief set an editing window, the day work could start is used instead. Each video says so in its note.
First draftThe first full edit posted for review. A-cuts, A-rolls and rough cuts do not count. For Saiyam it is the custom graphics version.
FinalThe day Sanket or Najma approved the last draft ("GTG" or equal). Changes made after approval do not move it. Where the thread stops at the last draft and the video is confirmed uploaded, the last draft date is used.
DaysWorking days, Monday to Friday, counting the day it was assigned as day 1. Monday to the next Monday is 6. Saturdays and Sundays are skipped, public holidays are not. Waiting on client assets, reshoots and review time are inside these numbers.
Client waitsIf the cut was finished or approved pending a client item (images, B-roll, screenshots, a recording), the video is dated to that day and the wait is not counted. Waits while the editor still lacked what they needed to finish stay in the days. After checking every thread, only 5 Levels of Marketing qualifies.
DraftsVersions posted from the first draft up to the one approved. A-cuts are not counted.
LengthOnly where someone wrote it in Slack: the editor, Najma, Gio or the brief. The source is in each note. Everything else says no data.
Due datesOnly dates written in Slack: deadlines in assignment messages, editing windows (Start and Deadline) and agreed extensions. Sanket wrote on 11 Nov that the date in an assignment message is when the final is wanted, so these count as final dates unless a video says otherwise. A red pill counts the working days after the date up to delivery, and a few dates stay "unclear".
Left outShorts, testimonials, social clips, promos, guides and videos picked up mid-edit are listed under other work. Anirba and Lokesh split one video, so it is shown but not averaged. Open videos count toward first-draft averages once a first draft exists.
Sources#internal-* video threads, #daily-update, one #trial-editor-lokesh message, one #core-team-hq message, and the Jeffrey Allen masterclass thread for the Sanket reference.

How this was counted
Plan daysSanket’s workflow is Wednesday to Wednesday, 6 working days for a 25 minute video: A-cut on day 1, 5, 15 and 25 minutes by days 2 to 4, review on day 5, V2 on day 6. Each further 10 minutes adds a day. The split of days 5 and 6 is assumed from the 6 day total. This is arithmetic, not a measurement.
Your ruleAbove 25 minutes add 1 day, above 30 add 2, then 1 per further full 10 minutes. A length written “25+” is read as just over 25.
